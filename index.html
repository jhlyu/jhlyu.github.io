Hey, I’m Jinghao, a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, physics, and computation.

Research

I study the energetic and physical limits of computation and learning. My work combines statistical physics, stochastic thermodynamics, and machine learning to understand how information-processing systems operate far from equilibrium and how they can be made more efficient.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between energy, information, computation, and intelligence, as well as data-driven methods for understanding and controlling complex dynamical systems.
